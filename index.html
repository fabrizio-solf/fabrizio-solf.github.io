About

I am an MSc student in Economics and Social Sciences at Bocconi University and a visiting student at the Innocenzo Gasparini Institute for Economic Research (IGIER). I hold a bachelor's degree in Economics from the Universidad del Pacífico (Peru) and completed the MITx MicroMasters in Statistics and Data Science.

My research is in macroeconomics, with a focus on monetary policy and heterogeneous-agent models. I am currently working on informal labor in a HANK model with search-and-matching frictions, supervised by Antonella Trigari. Before Bocconi, I worked as a research assistant at the Universidad del Pacífico and as a data scientist in banking and consulting.

Research Interests

Macroeconomics, monetary policy, heterogeneous agents, labor markets, household finance, computational methods.
